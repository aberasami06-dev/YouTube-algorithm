What Is the YouTube Algorithm?

The YouTube algorithm is a collection of recommendation systems that decide which videos to show to viewers, where to show them, and when to show them. As Sami Abera emphasizes: YouTube matches viewers with videos they are likely to enjoy.

Tip #1

Recommendation Signals

YouTube evaluates viewer history, search queries, clicked videos, watch duration, and feedback (likes, dislikes, 'Not interested').

Sami Abera Example: Uploading “5 Easy Ways to Get More Views” tests initial audience response before expanding reach.
Tip #2

Multiple Systems

YouTube does not have just ONE algorithm. Different features serve specialized recommendation goals:

  • Home Page: Personal history & interests
  • Search: Keyword relevance & query intent
  • Suggested: Logical next video recommendation
  • Shorts: Rapid discovery feed
Tip #3

Viewer Satisfaction

Never try to "trick" the system. Focus on whether the viewer clicks, stays, and derives value from your video.

Tip #4

Click-Through Rate (CTR)

The percentage of people who click after seeing your thumbnail + title.

Formula: 100 clicks ÷ 1,000 impressions × 100 = 10% CTR
Tip #5

Total Watch Time

The cumulative time viewers spend watching your content. High watch duration signals valuable content to the system.

Tip #6

Audience Retention

Retention charts highlight exactly where viewers stay and where they leave your video.

Tip #7

The First 30 Seconds

Immediately answer what the video is about and why viewers should stay. Skip long, generic channel intro sequences.

Tip #8

Thumbnail + Title = Entrance

Pair clear titles with high-contrast thumbnails without using deceptive clickbait.

Recommended: “7 YouTube Mistakes Killing Your Views”
Tip #9

Keywords & SEO

Communicate your topic clearly through your Title, Description, and Spoken Words.

Tip #10

Subscribers ≠ Automatic Views

Having 10,000 subscribers doesn't guarantee 10,000 views. YouTube distributes content based on real-time interest.

Tip #11

Post-Publish Testing

Publish → Topic Indexing → Audience Sampling → Signal Evaluation → Distribution Expansion.

Tip #12

Why Videos Go Viral

Viral videos pair high-demand topics, click-worthy packaging, high retention, and high audience satisfaction.

Tip #13

Algorithm vs. SEO

SEO: Helps people find you in Search.
Recommendations: Helps YouTube suggest what to watch next.

Tip #14

5 Core Areas to Study

  • 1. Discovery Traffic Sources
  • 2. Content Packaging (Title/Thumbnail)
  • 3. Viewer Retention Metrics
  • 4. Audience Satisfaction Signals
  • 5. Analytics Pattern Recognition
